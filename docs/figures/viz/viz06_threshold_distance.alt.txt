Log-log line chart of threshold distance against off-axis angle at the Wallops NEN dish. On boresight the short-term distance is 5.6 km, but a 400 ft drone can only reach the boresight of a 5 degree pass within 1.28 km. The distance falls to 298 m at 1 degree, 40 m at 5 degrees, and 7 m at 20 degrees. Gray lines show the long-term criterion and the 20 dB apportionment case.
